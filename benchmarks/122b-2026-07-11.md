## KV Cache Reality Check

KV pool is ~426K tokens. At 3 concurrent:
- 3 × 100K = 300K → fits ✅
- 3 × 142K = 426K → max capacity ✅
- 3 × 256K = 768K → exceeds pool ❌
- 2 × 213K = 426K → fits ✅

**Verified: 3 concurrent at 100K each is the realistic max. 256K requires 1-2 concurrent.**
