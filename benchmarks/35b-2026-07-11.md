**Key finding:** MTP k=3 maintains 60-86% acceptance across all 3 positions. Compare to DFlash n=12 which collapses to <5% after position 3 on agent/prose traffic. MTP k=3 doesn't have the prose collapse problem because it only drafts 3 tokens — less wasted bandwidth on rejections.
