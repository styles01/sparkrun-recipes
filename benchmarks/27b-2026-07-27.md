## Notes

- Thinking ON produces higher t/s because more tokens generated per request (reasoning + answer)
- Thinking OFF is faster per-request but lower aggregate t/s due to short completions
- MTP k=7 testing in progress
- GMU 0.70 frees 21GB vs 0.85 for co-located workloads
- sparkrun `docker exec` / OCI setns issue prevents sparkrun from managing this container on this host; direct `docker run` works fine
